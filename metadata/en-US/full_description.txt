Lipi is a private, open-source document scanner. Point the camera at a page and Lipi finds its edges, flattens it, cleans it up, and saves it as a PDF or image.

Everything happens on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no trackers, and the app needs no network permission.

Planned features:
- Live page detection with an on-device model, and auto-capture
- Batch scanning, manual corner adjustment with a magnifier, shadow removal, and Auto, B&W, Gray and Color filters
- PDF and JPG export
- Text recognition (OCR) for searchable PDFs, including Bengali and Hindi, with downloadable language packs

Lipi is free software under the MIT license. The source code, models and training scripts are all public.
